- Free Tier
    Some services free forever. Some free for 12 months

- On-demand model
    If you ask for a vm of a certain size, you're going to pay for that size and duration
    EX: 2 processor, 16 GB, for 10 hours - pay for just that

- Reservartions
    Commit to paying for a certain amount of time and get a deep discount

- Volume Discounts
    Get discounts as you consume more. Lower cost per unit the more you use

- Price Drops
    Will drop prices for services as they get used to running them. Have dropped prices 100+ times since inception
