# Cloud economics

## Free Tier

- Some services are free forever.
- Some services are free for 12 months.

## On-demand model

If you ask for a virtual machine of a certain size, you pay for that size and for how long it runs.

Example: 2 processors and 16 GB for 10 hours. You pay for just that.

## Reservations

Commit to paying for a certain amount of time and get a deep discount.

## Volume discounts

Get discounts as you consume more. The cost per unit goes down the more you use.

## Price drops

AWS drops prices for services as they get used to running them. Prices have been dropped more than 100 times since the service launched.
